## Description (4000) — 1937 characters
Pick your balls, aim the launch and watch them brawl!

Ball Brawl is a fast, bouncy arena battler. Every ball has its own wild way to fight: shoot bolts, ram with spikes, drop lava, cast spells, open portals, rewind time and much more. Build a team, launch into the arena and let physics do the rest.

33+ BALLS TO COLLECT
• Each ball fights differently: Blaster, Spike, Magma, Wizard, Portal, Glitch, Clockwork, Fibonacci, Twin and many more
• Find new balls in chests and level them up to level 15 with cards and coins
• Unlock a Perk, a Side Ability and a powerful Ultimate as they level up
• Watch a live preview of every ability before you upgrade

RANKED 3V3 BATTLES
• Take your best team of 3 into ranked online battles
• Pick who goes in each round. Health carries over, so every choice counts
• Win trophies and climb the Trophy Road through 6 themed arenas
• Collect coins, cards and chests at reward stops along the way

MORE WAYS TO PLAY
• Online: quick matches and private rooms to battle your friends
• VS Bot: 3v3 series against the computer, perfect for practice
• 2 Players: battle a friend on the same device
• Daily Crusade: lead a team of 3 across adventure maps in Normal and Hard mode. Fallen balls rest until tomorrow!

FREE REWARDS EVERY DAY
• 7-day login streak with growing rewards
• Daily quests and a bonus chest for finishing them all
• A free chest every few hours
• Level up your player profile for even more chests

FEATURES
• Quick battles that are easy to learn and fun to master
• Bright cartoon art with juicy hits, K.O.s and big Ultimate moments
• Your progress syncs across your devices with iCloud
• Game Center leaderboards and friend invites
• Play in English, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French and Romanian

Ball Brawl is free to play. Optional in-app purchases are available, and you can choose to watch short ads for extra rewards.
