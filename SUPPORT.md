## Frequently asked questions

**How do I play online or ranked?**
Online and ranked battles need Game Center. Open iPhone Settings › Game Center, sign in, then come back to the game. VS Bot, 2 Players, the Crusade and your Collection all work without it.

**My progress isn't on my new device.**
Progress syncs through iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, open the game on both devices, and give it a moment to sync.

**I bought something but didn't get it.**
Go to Shop › Restore Purchases. If it still doesn't show up, email us with the date of the purchase (you can find it in Settings › [your name] › Media & Purchases › View Account › Purchase History).

**How do I use a voucher code?**
Go to Settings › Redeem Code and type the code. Each code can be used once per player. Some codes need you to be signed in to iCloud.

**How do I change my ad privacy choices?**
In the EU, UK and some other regions, open Settings › Ad Privacy Choices. Everywhere, you can control tracking in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

**How do I start over?**
Settings › Reset Progress erases your progress on all your devices. Your name, purchases and used voucher codes are kept. This can't be undone.

**I found a bug or have an idea.**
We'd love to hear it. Email us at asar.innovations@gmail.com.
